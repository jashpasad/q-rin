Q-RIN: pricing and paying down quantum "harvest debt" in finance

Adversaries can record encrypted traffic and steal encrypted archives today, then decrypt them once a quantum computer exists. Every month a bank keeps RSA or elliptic-curve cryptography on a channel, it adds to that pile. Q-RIN (ऋण, debt) measures this accruing liability, plans the migration order that minimises it, and devalues data that may already be gone.

The threat

The cost of breaking today's cryptography keeps falling

Published resource estimates for factoring RSA-2048 have dropped by four orders of magnitude in fourteen years. Breaking 256-bit elliptic curves, used in most TLS handshakes, now needs about 1,200 logical qubits.

Why this is a loss today, not in 2035

Data captured at time t stays valuable for its secrecy lifetime L. It is lost if the quantum computer arrives before t + L. Biometric identity data never expires, so every capture counts.

How Q-RIN works

Five stages: discover, price, plan, devalue, supervise

Q-RIN sits on top of existing crypto-inventory tools. It adds the decision layer they lack: a price on delay and the order of fixes that minimises total loss.

01 · Discover

Harvest Lens

  • TLS 1.3 key-share group (X25519 vs X25519MLKEM768), read in cleartext from handshakes
  • IKE/IPsec and SSH key-exchange proposals
  • PGP and CMS headers in file stores and logs
  • X.509, HSM and KMS key inventories
Decrypts nothing · Output: CycloneDX CBOM
02 · Price

Debt engine

  • Maps each flow or archive to a data class and secrecy lifetime
  • Volume × value × harvest probability
  • × probability the quantum computer arrives before the secret expires
Output: harvest-debt ledger, debt rate per system
03 · Plan

Scheduler

  • Weighted-completion-time scheduling
  • Dependencies: HSM, PKI, key-wrap; both ends of a channel
  • Parallel teams, budget
  • Robust across arrival scenarios (minimax regret)
Output: quarter-by-quarter migration plan
04 · Devalue

Playbook

  • Hybrid ML-KEM tunnels on highest-debt links
  • Re-wrap or crypto-shred RSA-wrapped archives
  • Rotate secrets that crossed vulnerable channels
Output: quick wins that stop accrual within months
05 · Supervise

Sector view

  • Aggregated debt and burn-down per institution
  • Two-ended channel coordination (NPCI, UIDAI, SFMS)
  • Concentration on shared vendors
Output: outcome metric for RBI, SEBI, IFSCA

The harvest-debt equation

D_j = ∫₀^{C_j} r_j · F(t + L_j) dt total debt = Σ_j D_j r_j harvestable value per month flow: records/month × value/record × P(path recorded) archive: stored records × value/record × P(store stolen per month) L_j secrecy lifetime of the data class (years) F probability the quantum computer has arrived by a given time C_j month when system j's migration completes

Debt accrues until migration completes. A month of delay on a high-rate system costs more than a month on a low-rate one, which is why order matters.

Why order matters: a two-system example

One team. System A accrues 100 units/month and takes 2 months. System B accrues 300 units/month and takes 12 months.

A first: 100×2 + 300×14 = 4,400. B first: 300×12 + 100×14 = 5,000. Smith's rule (rate ÷ effort: A = 50, B = 25) picks A first and is provably optimal for one team without dependencies. Intuition says fix the bigger risk first; the maths says otherwise.

What the scheduler adds beyond Smith's rule

  • Dependencies. An HSM or key-management upgrade unlocks several systems. It carries no debt itself but inherits the weight of what it unlocks.
  • Two-ended channels. UPI credential encryption is safe only when NPCI and the PSP both migrate. Ecosystem mode schedules across institutions.
  • Parallel teams. List scheduling across teams, improved by local search.
  • Uncertain Q-day. One plan is optimised for the scenario-weighted expectation and checked for regret against each scenario's own best plan.
  • Re-planning. When resource estimates fall, the arrival curve shifts and the plan updates.

Devalue

Make what is stolen worth less

Data already recorded cannot be un-recorded. Q-RIN's playbook stops new accrual fast and shrinks what a future breach could yield.

weeks

Hybrid tunnels

Put X25519MLKEM768 or IPsec with ML-KEM on the highest-debt links first. Legacy applications stay untouched behind the tunnel.

weeks

Re-wrap archives

Re-encrypt PGP files, logs and backups under ML-KEM or symmetric-only keys, crypto-shred RSA-wrapped copies, and delete what retention rules no longer require.

months

Rotate secrets

Rotate API keys, long-lived credentials and keys that crossed vulnerable channels, so harvested copies expire before a quantum computer exists.

ecosystem

Redesign permanent secrets

Biometrics cannot be rotated. Flag them for protocol-level PQC key transport with the identity and payment operators.

Method and sources

Assumptions, limits and references

Simulation setup

  • 35 systems: 27 data flows, 5 encrypted archives, 3 enabling upgrades (HSM, PKI, KMS key-wrap).
  • 4 parallel teams; list scheduling with precedence; 60-month horizon on a monthly grid.
  • Arrival scenarios: lognormal with medians of 4.5, 13 and 22 years, weighted 25/50/25.
  • Q-RIN plan: Smith-ratio seed, then 6,000 swap and insertion moves on expected debt.
  • Robustness: volumes ×÷3, values ×÷2, harvest probability and effort ±50%, redrawn for 120 banks.

Limits

  • Parameters are synthetic. Real calibration needs metadata-only inventories from institutions.
  • Value is in relative units. Only the order matters, and randomising values does not remove the effect.
  • Harvest probabilities are path-class estimates; the pilot will refine them.
  • Signature forgery after Q-day ("trust now, forge later") is a separate metric, planned as a second module.
